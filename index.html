Portugal, together

Charlie & parents · Lisbon + Porto + Aveiro · Oct 28 – Nov 5, 2026 · Draft v15 · Sep 9 — Regaleira 10:00 + Pena 15:00 + Jerónimos 9:30 Oct 29 + Lello 9:00 Nov 2 + Porto→Aveiro 11:40 + Aveiro→Lisbon 15:25 Nov 3 all booked

Lisbon 3n→Porto 3n→Aveiro stop→Lisbon 2n ✅

The shape: Round-trip into Lisbon means the last night has to be back in Lisbon for your 11am flight home. So: Lisbon first, a scenic transfer day north with Óbidos, three easy days around Porto, a 2-3hr flat stop in Aveiro on the way back south to break up the train, then a relaxed finish in Baixa. Last 2n at Convent Square is booked.

Pace: Built for people who enjoy walking but not marathons — most days cap out at a few gentle hours on foot, and hills get handled with trams, tuk-tuks, Ubers, and guided transport.

Day by day

Wed Oct 28

Arrival, easy Lisbon

Very light walkingBook: early check-in + tuk-tuk

Land 8:00am LIS. Bolt to hotel — request early check-in now or book the previous night so the room is ready. Drop bags, slow coffee and a first pastel de nata.

Your list for today: Praça do Comércio, lunch, Rua Augusta, Rossio, Alfama, and Castelo de São Jorge. Flat loop first — Praça → Rua Augusta → Rossio — then a two-hour private tuk-tuk covers Alfama lanes and miradouros (Portas do Sol, Santa Luzia, Graça) seated.

Castle is the hilltop finale. If jet-lag hits, leave it — we'll move it to Tue or Wed in Lisbon. Early dinner, early night.

Thu Oct 29

Belém & Cascais — seaside afternoon ✅ Jerónimos 9:30 BOOKED

Moderate — flat but full day✅ Jerónimos 9:30 BOOKED + Cascais train

Morning in Belém — your whole list sits on a flat line along the river:

  • Jerónimos Monastery ✅ BOOKED 9:30am Thu Oct 29 — first wave, queue grows fast.
  • Pastéis de Belém straight after, still warm. The takeaway window or the big back rooms beat the outside line.
  • Monument to the Discoveries along the riverside promenade — an elevator runs to the top viewpoint, no stairs needed.
  • Torre de Belém ten minutes further along the flat walkway. The classic view is from the promenade — go inside only if everyone feels spry (narrow spiral stairs).

Afternoon — Cascais — Bolt 15 min from Belém to Cais do Sodré, then train Cais do Sodré → Cascais 38 min avg, every 30 min, left side for river views. Flat seaside stroll, Boca do Inferno viewpoint, last seafood lunch / ice cream. Train back ~5pm, early night. Fado moved to Tue Nov 3.

Fri Oct 30

Sintra — DIY, Regaleira 10am + Pena 3pm ✅ BOTH BOOKED

Moderate walking — Bolt between hills✅ BOOKED: Regaleira 10:00 + Pena 15:00

The idea: DIY with train + Bolt — no 434 bus. Regaleira first hour when quietest, then flat historic center lunch, then Moorish exterior viewpoint + Pena Palace 15:00 (sunset 5:38pm, you’ll have daylight for palace + descent). Bolt between hills saves steps for parents.

  • 8:15am Rossio → Sintra train (~40 min). Be on platform 8:00. From Sintra station 10-min walk or Bolt to Quinta da Regaleira.
  • 10:00-11:45 Quinta da Regaleira ✅ BOOKED 10:00 — Initiation Well first (queue builds), then tunnels/gardens, then palace. Rent €5 audio devices at ticket office (30 points, EN) or use phone audio app. Uneven paths + damp tunnels, go slow. 1h45m.
  • 11:45-1:00pm Historic center — seated lunch — 10-min flat walk to town. Travesseiro at Piriquita, slow lunch, azulejo shops.
  • ~1:00pm Bolt toward Pena/Moorish area (~15 min up).
  • ~1:20-1:35 Moorish Castle — exterior/viewpoint only — ask driver to pause at Miradouro viewpoint/parking before castle turnoff for full wall line + Pena above, zero steps. 10-15 min photo, skip ticket/walls — walls are continuous stone steps, exposed, windy.
  • ~1:45-2:45 Pena Park buffer + arrive early — gate entry (park entry anytime, palace time strict). Shuttle up from gate to palace (saves 15-min steep walk). Ticket says arrive 15 min early — be at palace by 2:45 for 3:00 entry. Light jacket — Sintra cooler/foggier than Lisbon.
  • 3:00-4:30 Pena Palace ✅ BOOKED 15:00 — interiors 45-60 min, terraces 20-30 min for views. Terrace moody even if fog rolls in.
  • ~4:30-5:15 Shuttle down to gate, Bolt to Sintra station.
  • ~5:15-6:00 Sintra → Lisbon train (~40 min). Back Rossio ~6:00, Baixa hotel ~6:30. Dusk on descent but still daylight in palace.

Logistics: Bolt/Uber works at Pena exit and in town — buses packed 30-60min lines. Pena ticket reschedulable online until 6pm Thu Oct 29 — keep for fog backup. Regaleira also reschedulable per your booking — check confirmation email for reschedule link/deadline, first hour + Well first is quietest. No Cabo da Roca — Nazaré covers big Atlantic views.

Sat Oct 31

Private transfer to Porto via Óbidos, Nazaré & Batalha — ✅ BOOKED Daytrip

Light walking — long driving day✅ BOOKED: Daytrip private transfer

✅ BOOKED Daytrip — Private driver, door-to-door Lisbon → Porto. Sat Oct 31, 8:00 AM pickup at Hotel Santa Justa, ETA 5:27 PM Cale Guest House. Includes 3 stops as booked.

  • 8:00 AM Pickup — Hotel Santa Justa Lisboa, Rua dos Correeiros, Lisbon
  • 9:12 AM - 11:12 AM Óbidos — 120 min — Porta da Vila, Rua Direita to Igreja de Santa Maria, ginjinha, castle exterior. Wall walk ~20 min out-and-back only from Porta da Vila staircase "Muralhas" if steady — uneven, no rail, skip if wet/crowded. Full circuit is 1.5km+ and not parent-friendly.
  • 11:46 AM - 1:46 PM Nazaré — 120 min — Lunch at Restaurante Sitiado in Sítio (aim 12:00-12:30pm reservation for 3). Driver drops at Sítio (upper town). Suberco viewpoint, Ermida da Memória, Forte de São Miguel Arcanjo / Praia do Norte view before/after lunch. Skip lower town unless you want funicular novelty (2 min ride, ~10-15 min frequency, ~20-25 min round trip).
  • 2:15 PM - 3:30 PM Batalha — 75 min — Batalha Monastery, nave, Founder's Chapel, Unfinished Chapels, quick cloister/Chapter House if energy. Flat, benches inside.
  • ~5:27 PM Arrive — Cale Guest House, Largo de São Domingos, Porto. Check-in, easy evening along Ribeira.

Order is Óbidos → Nazaré → Batalha as routed by Daytrip — not the most efficient (Batalha before Nazaré would save backtracking) but keeps Nazaré at lunch time. Total driving ~4.5-5h + 5h15m stops = ~9.5h door-to-door. Parent-friendly, vehicle at each stop.

Mon Nov 2

Porto at its own pace — ✅ Lello 9:00 BOOKED

Some walking✅ BOOKED: Lello 9:00 + cellar tour

Museums mostly rest on Mondays, but your Porto list doesn't — perfect day for Lello early:

  • 9:00-9:45 Livraria Lello ✅ BOOKED 9:00 — be there 8:50 for first entry, 30-45 min inside, voucher price comes off a book so a souvenir is built in. Early = empty staircase for photos.
  • Drift downhill through the old streets to Ribeira do Porto for a waterfront lunch.
  • Cross the Luís I Bridge on the flat lower deck to Gaia — Cálem cellar tour ✅ BOOKED Mon Nov 2, 2:40 PM, 3 adults, guided tour + interactive museum + 3 tastings (English), $80.04 total, free cancellation until Nov 1. Tour ~1 hr, ends ~3:40.
  • Late afternoon: Foz do Douro for sunset (~5:30 PM). Walk back across the bridge to Infante and ride the wood-paneled Tram 1 along the riverbank to Passeio Alegre (~25 min, ~€6, tickets on board, board at Infante for seats) — scenery without walking — or Bolt/Uber. Lighthouse, beach promenade, then Bolt back after dinner (don't rely on the tram for a late return).

Dinner in Ribeira, or a short Uber to Matosinhos for the best grilled fish on the coast.

Wed Nov 4

Lisbon flex — Tram 28 + easy finish

As much as you like — Tram is seatedGo early to skip lines

Morning — Tram 28, requested by your parents. Board at Martim Moniz start of line to get seats, ideally 9-9:30am before lines build. Ride the best scenic half: Martim Moniz → Graça → Alfama → Baixa (~20 min). Steps are high/narrow, grab rail needed. Right side going up has better views. Get off at Baixa-Chiado — clean exit, flat.

Mid-morning — pick one easy finish — all parent-friendly, low walking:

  • Tile Museum (Museu do Azulejo) — flat, quiet, stunning chapel covered in blue tiles. Bolt 10 min from Baixa, 1 hr seated. Great final souvenir shop.
  • Alfama + Castelo top-up — if you skipped Castelo de São Jorge on arrival day (Wed Oct 28), do it now with tuk-tuk support. Miradouros, then A Vida Portuguesa for gifts.
  • Time Out Market + riverside — super light option, food hall grazing, Praça do Comércio sunset, pack for 11am flight.

Cascais already done Thu Oct 29, so today is slow and flat — no trains, just Baixa base. Fado was Tue Nov 3.
