Portugal, together

Charlie & parents · Lisbon + Porto + Aveiro · Oct 28 – Nov 5, 2026 · draft v6

Lisbon 3n→Porto 3n→Aveiro stop→Lisbon 2n ✅

The shape: Round-trip into Lisbon means the last night has to be back in Lisbon for your 11am flight home. So: Lisbon first, a scenic transfer day north with Óbidos, three easy days around Porto, a 2-3hr flat stop in Aveiro on the way back south to break up the train, then a relaxed finish in Baixa. Last 2n at Convent Square is booked.

Pace: Built for people who enjoy walking but not marathons — most days cap out at a few gentle hours on foot, and hills get handled with trams, tuk-tuks, Ubers, and guided transport.

Day by day

Wed Oct 28

Arrival, easy Lisbon

Very light walkingBook: early check-in + tuk-tuk

Land 8:00am LIS. Bolt to hotel — request early check-in now or book the previous night so the room is ready. Drop bags, slow coffee and a first pastel de nata.

Your list for today: Praça do Comércio, lunch, Rua Augusta, Rossio, Alfama, and Castelo de São Jorge. Flat loop first — Praça → Rua Augusta → Rossio — then a two-hour private tuk-tuk covers Alfama lanes and miradouros (Portas do Sol, Santa Luzia, Graça) seated.

Castle is the hilltop finale. If jet-lag hits, leave it — we'll move it to Tue or Wed in Lisbon. Early dinner, early night.

Thu Oct 29

Belém & a fado evening

Light, flat walkingBook: fado dinner

Morning in Belém — your whole list sits on a flat line along the river:

  • Jerónimos Monastery around the 9:30 opening — the queue grows fast, first wave wins.
  • Pastéis de Belém straight after, still warm. The takeaway window or the big back rooms beat the outside line.
  • Monument to the Discoveries along the riverside promenade — an elevator runs to the top viewpoint, no stairs needed.
  • Torre de Belém ten minutes further along the flat walkway. The interior is narrow spiral staircases; the classic view is honestly from the promenade — go inside only if everyone feels spry.

Back for a mid-afternoon rest. Evening: a fado dinner house in Alfama — three hours seated with food and music, a short Uber up the hill, reserve ahead.

Fri Oct 30

Sintra, all to itself

The fullest walking dayBook: Pena 9:30 slot

Train Lisbon Rossio → Sintra ( ~40 min). Then Bolt / taxi between the hills — faster than the 434 bus on a busy day.

  • Pena Palace first, earliest slot (~9:30) — shuttle up from park gate, terraces, interiors.
  • Moorish Castle next door — walls are continuous stone steps, decide on the day if legs are still fresh.
  • Historic center for slow lunch + travesseiro at Casa Piriquita.
  • Quinta da Regaleira last — gardens and grottos, Initiation Well optional.

It's a lot, so start early. Bolt back to Sintra station, train to Lisbon.

Sat Oct 31

Private transfer to Porto via Óbidos, Batalha & Nazaré

Light walking — long driving dayBook: private car transfer

Private driver transfer Lisbon → Porto with stops. Rough timing: Lisbon 8:30am → Óbidos ~1hr, 1.5hr wander (walled town, ginjinha) → Batalha Monastery ~45min drive, 1hr inside → Nazaré ~30min, viewpoint + lunch → Porto Ribeira ~2hr, arrive ~5-6pm.

Check into Porto, evening along Ribeira. It's a full day, so tell the driver if you want to drop one stop.

Sun Nov 1

Douro Valley wine day

Very light walkingBook: wine tour

All Saint's Day is a public holiday, which makes it the ideal day to be out of town. Full-day guided Douro tour: the famously scenic valley drive, tastings at two quintas, a riverside winery lunch, and a gentle hour on a boat. Door-to-door from your hotel, no driving worries.

Mon Nov 2

Porto at its own pace

Some walkingBook: Lello voucher, cellar tour

Museums mostly rest on Mondays, but your Porto list doesn't:

  • Livraria Lello on a timed morning voucher — book ahead, and the voucher price comes off a book, so a souvenir is built in.
  • Drift downhill through the old streets to Ribeira do Porto for a waterfront lunch.
  • Cross the Luís I Bridge on the flat lower deck to Gaia for an afternoon port cellar tour and tasting — Calem or Sandeman sit right on the river. The Gaia cable car runs up beside the bridge if anyone wants the big view without the climb.
  • Late afternoon: Foz do Douro, where the river meets the ocean. The wood-paneled Tram 1 runs there from Ribeira along the riverbank — scenery without walking — or it's a ten-minute Uber. Sunset, lighthouse, coffee by the sea.

Dinner in Ribeira, or a short Uber to Matosinhos for the best grilled fish on the coast.

Tue Nov 3

Porto → Aveiro → Lisbon

Very light, flat walkingBook: 2 CP trains + bag storage

Unhurried Porto morning: São Bento station azulejos, last coffee at Majestic Café, check out.

Train 1: Porto-Campanhã → Aveiro (~34 min fast Alfa/Intercidades, or ~1hr regional). Trains every ~30 min, direct.

Aveiro stop — 2.5-3 hrs: Totally flat, parent-friendly. Store bags — station has Lockers / Luggage Storage on the facilities list; backup Bounce shop 2-min across the street if lockers full or not working. Canal loop on foot, moliceiro boat 45-min ride (seated, no walking), ovos moles tasting at Confeitaria Peixinho, Art Nouveau facades along the canal. No hills.

Train 2: Aveiro → Lisbon Oriente or Santa Apolónia (~2h15–2h45). Arrive early evening. Walk/Bolt 10 min to Convent Square Lisbon (Baixa, flat, pedestrian lane, ✅ BOOKED).

Evening in Lisbon — light re-walk for anything you skipped: Praça do Comércio / Rua Augusta or deeper Alfama. Easy dinner near hotel.

Wed Nov 4

Lisbon flex + Tram 28 + Cascais

As much as you like — Tram is seatedGo early to skip lines

Morning — Tram 28, requested by your parents. Board at Martim Moniz start of line to get seats, ideally 9-9:30am before lines build. Ride the best scenic half: Martim Moniz → Graça → Alfama → Baixa (~20 min). Steps are high/narrow, grab rail needed. Right side going up has better views.

For your parents’ plan, get off at Baixa-Chiado — past that you climb to Estrela/Campo Ourique which is residential and adds 15-20 min with no extra Alfama. Baixa-Chiado is the clean exit for Cascais.

To Cascais: From Baixa-Chiado, Bolt 3 min or Blue metro 1 stop to Cais do Sodré, then train Cais do Sodré → Cascais 38 min average, every 30 min, direct via Comboios de Portugal. Flat seaside ride, left side going out for river views. Mid-morning arrival → flat seaside stroll, last seafood lunch, pack for 11am flight.

Loose mid-morning fallback: A Vida Portuguesa, Ajuda Palace (flat, quieter museum), Time Out Market if time remains.

Thu Nov 5

Fly home

Departure

11:00am flight — leave for the airport around 8:00am (about 20–25 minutes by Uber from Baixa). Check in is calm at that hour, but three hours early is the right call for a transatlantic flight.

Where to stay

Lisbon — Baixa — ✅ BOOKED Convent Square (Nov 3-5)

Convent Square Lisbon, Vignette Collection by IHG, 2 nights Nov 3-5. Baixa flat base, pedestrian-only lane off Rossio, 1-min walk from Uber drop-off, easy for parents. Grand Room 2 Twin Beds sleeps 3. Central, flat, metro at the doorstep.

Porto — Aliados / Bolhão, or Gaia riverfront

Aliados keeps you central on gentler ground. Gaia's waterfront is flat, has the best views of Porto, and puts the cellars next door.
